CONCLUSION. Laurent's decomposition is a statement about BINARY continuous-phase
modulation. SOQPSK's excitation is ternary by definition, so the pulse OpenVSA
would shape and match does not describe the signal, and no choice of pulse fixes
it -- the control shows the same construction exact on a binary alphabet and
wrong by 14 %rms on the ternary one, on the same pulse. Demodulating SOQPSK needs
either a detector over the modulation's phase states, which the 14-step chain of
REQ-DEM-001 has no place for, or the ternary PAM decomposition, whose pulse comes
from the literature rather than from this standard. Both are decisions rather than
implementations, so the catalogue refuses the name and says which.
