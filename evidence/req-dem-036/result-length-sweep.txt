Does a Result Length below the format's recommendation break carrier lock?
=========================================================================

Measured 2026-08-24 against the chain at 77125c1, through OpenVSA.Demod.Tests.

Fixture
    symbol rate      1 MHz
    sample rate      16 MHz
    shaping          root raised cosine, alpha 0.35, 20 symbols span
    measurement      root raised cosine, alpha 0.35 -- matched
    carrier offset   30 kHz, so that step 8 has a frequency to estimate
    seed             20260824
    record           4000 symbols

"Locked" is LockDiagnosis's own judgement: EVM below 25 %rms. The recommendation is
Constellation.RecommendedResultLengthSymbols = max(50, points).

Reading
-------

56 of 56 locked. The worst reading anywhere in the sweep is 12.14 %rms, half the 25 %
that separates a poor demodulation from one that is not locked -- and it is at 15 dB on a
128-point cross constellation, where 7.6 %rms is what the SAME format reads at the full
recommended length. Most of that is the noise, not the window.

The EVM does not even fall monotonically with the window: 256-QAM at 15 dB reads 6.25 %rms
at 256 symbols and 3.17 %rms at 4. A four-symbol window is not a better measurement; it is
a measurement of four symbols, whose EVM is the spread of four points and not of the
format. That is the sense in which the Result Length is too short -- unrepresentative, not
unlocked.

So REQ-DEM-036's fourth cause cannot be injected in isolation against this chain, and the
acceptance test injects it alongside a filter mismatch that does break the lock. See #429.


A note on what was NOT swept
----------------------------

pi/4-DQPSK was in an earlier version of this sweep and was removed: the fixture was wrong
rather than the chain. A generator given the rotation but not told to encode differentially
transmits something the demodulator is not decoding, and the chain reported 43-47 %rms at
every Result Length including the full one. The diagnosis blamed the centre frequency,
which is CORRECT -- an unhandled rotation of pi/4 per symbol is indistinguishable from a
carrier offset of one eighth of the symbol rate, and it said so. That is worth recording as
a case the diagnostic gets right for the right reason.
